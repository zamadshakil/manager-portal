# Q3 2025 Performance Report

## Executive Summary

This report summarises the operational performance of the Alpha Team for the period July through September 2025. The team met or exceeded all primary KPIs and delivered two strategic projects ahead of schedule. Overall performance is rated as satisfactory with specific areas identified for improvement in Q4.

## KPI Breakdown

| KPI | Target | Achieved | Status |
|-----|--------|----------|--------|
| Client Satisfaction Score | 80% | 84% | ✅ Met |
| Task Completion Rate | 90% | 93% | ✅ Met |
| SLA Compliance | 95% | 91% | ⚠️ Near miss |
| Cost Reduction | 10% | 12% | ✅ Exceeded |
| Staff Retention | 85% | 88% | ✅ Met |

## Challenges

The primary challenge encountered this quarter was resource contention between the infrastructure audit workstream and the new client onboarding initiative. These two efforts competed for the same three senior analysts, causing temporary delays in the onboarding documentation phase.

A secondary challenge was the delayed delivery of third-party API credentials required for the inventory integration project. This dependency was outside the team's control but resulted in a two-week slip on the integration testing phase.

## Next Quarter Targets

1. Resolve the SLA compliance gap by implementing the new escalation workflow by 15 October 2025.
2. Complete cross-training of two junior analysts on the inventory system to reduce key-person dependency.
3. Finalise and sign off the Q4 capacity plan by 30 October 2025 to ensure headcount is in place before the peak season.

## Conclusion

The Alpha Team has delivered a solid Q3 with measurable improvement across most KPIs. The SLA near-miss is the primary focus area heading into Q4 and has been escalated to the operations director for additional resourcing support. The team remains on track for the full-year performance targets.
