Q3 report for operations

ok so this quarter was honestly pretty mixed. like we did some good stuff but also things went wrong a lot. the vendor thing finally got sorted which was great because it was dragging on forever and everyone was fed up with it. we probably saved some money on that which is good i guess.

the client satisfaction thing went up which was nice, people seemed happier this quarter compared to last time. the account manager idea worked out better than expected even though at first half the team thought it was a waste of time and too much extra work.

the SLA thing is still a problem though. we missed the target again and honestly it was because of the new system rollout which kind of broke everything for a couple weeks. the IT guys said it would be fine but it wasnt really fine. people were complaining in the group chat every day about stuff not working properly. eventually it got better but the damage was already done for the metrics.

resource wise we were stretched really thin. like three people doing the work of five which is not sustainable at all. the audit and the onboarding thing were both happening at the same time and nobody really thought about whether we had enough people for both. classic planning fail.

going forward we need to fix the SLA stuff obviously. also we should probably cross train some of the junior staff because right now if two specific people are out sick everything falls apart which is a massive risk. and the Q4 plan needs to be ready soon because december is always crazy busy and if we dont have the right headcount sorted in advance its going to be a disaster like last year.

thats basically it for Q3. overall an ok quarter but room for improvement in pretty much everything.
