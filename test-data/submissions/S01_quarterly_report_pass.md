# Operations Team — Q3 2025 Performance Report

**Submitted by:** Operations Team  
**Period:** July 1 – September 30, 2025  
**Prepared for:** Senior Management Review

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Operations Team delivered a strong third quarter, meeting or exceeding four of five primary KPIs. The most significant achievement was the early completion of the vendor consolidation project, which is projected to reduce procurement costs by 14% over the next fiscal year. One area of concern — SLA compliance — dipped to 91% against a target of 95%, driven by a two-week disruption caused by the transition to the new inventory tracking system. Corrective measures have been identified and are described in Section 4.

Overall, Q3 demonstrated the team's capacity to manage parallel workstreams under resource constraints while maintaining client satisfaction above benchmark levels.

## 3. Challenges

### 3.1 Resource Contention

The primary operational challenge this quarter was the overlap between the infrastructure audit workstream and the new client onboarding initiative. Both efforts competed for three senior analysts who possessed the technical expertise required for each project. This created periodic bottlenecks that delayed the onboarding documentation phase by approximately eight business days.

To address this, the team will implement a dedicated resource allocation matrix in Q4 that assigns analysts to primary and secondary workstreams with explicit escalation paths when conflicts arise.

### 3.2 Third-Party Dependency Delays

The delayed delivery of API credentials from a key integration partner caused a two-week slip in the inventory system testing phase. This delay was outside the team's direct control; however, the incident highlighted the need for earlier vendor engagement during project planning. A formal SLA agreement with external integration partners will be pursued in Q4.

### 3.3 SLA Compliance Dip

The 4-point shortfall in SLA compliance was directly attributable to the inventory system migration period. Ticket resolution times increased during the transition as support staff adapted to the new interface. Average resolution time returned to baseline within two weeks of go-live.

---

## 4. Next Quarter Targets

1. **Restore SLA Compliance to 95%** — Implement the new ticket escalation workflow by October 15, 2025. Assign two senior analysts as dedicated escalation owners for high-priority tickets.

2. **Complete Cross-Training Programme** — Train two junior analysts on the full inventory management system by November 1, 2025. This eliminates the key-person dependency identified in Q3.

3. **Finalise Q4 Capacity Plan** — Submit the approved headcount and budget plan to the operations director by October 30, 2025 to ensure resources are in place before the peak season in December.

---

## 5. Conclusion

Q3 2025 was a productive quarter for the Operations Team, with clear progress on strategic initiatives and measurable improvement in client-facing metrics. The SLA compliance gap is the primary focus area for Q4 and has been escalated to the operations director. With the corrective measures outlined above, the team is well-positioned to meet full-year performance targets.
