## Post 2 — Measure a week first

A review-time estimate starts with a few ordinary numbers: submissions per week, minutes per review and how often work comes back for another pass.

That baseline is more useful than a savings promise before anyone has seen the workflow.

We've included a local calculator and a workflow worksheet in the Hierarchia showcase. The numbers are yours to explore; they're estimates, not evidence of product savings.
