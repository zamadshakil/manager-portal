## 2. Recurring Themes

### Theme A — Delivery Speed (mentioned in 127 reviews, 36.5%)
The single most discussed topic. Positive reviews praise next-day delivery;
negative reviews cite delays of 4–7 days on standard shipping orders.

> *"My order arrived the very next morning — absolutely blown away by how fast that was!"* — Emma T., Trustpilot ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
> *"Paid for standard shipping and waited 8 days. Not good enough."* — Anonymous, Google Reviews ⭐⭐

### Theme B — Product Quality & Packaging (mentioned in 98 reviews, 28.2%)
Customers consistently praise the premium feel of product packaging.
A subset of reviews from ELEC-3301 buyers flagged that the headphone padding
feels thin compared to the product photos.

> *"The packaging was beautiful — felt like opening a luxury gift. The tote bag quality is exactly as described."* — Priya S., Website Review ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
> *"The headphone ear cushions look much thicker in the photos than in reality. Bit disappointed."* — James K., Website Review ⭐⭐⭐

### Theme C — Customer Support Responsiveness (mentioned in 74 reviews, 21.3%)
Customers who contacted support gave mixed feedback. Live chat users rated
support 4.6/5 on average; email support users rated it 3.1/5 citing long
response times (avg. 48h vs. stated 24h SLA).

> *"Chat support solved my exchange request in under 5 minutes. Impressive."* — Liam N., Post-purchase Survey
> *"Sent two emails and waited 3 days with no reply. Had to call."* — Aisha O., Trustpilot ⭐⭐

## 4. Actionable Recommendations

1. **Update ELEC-3301 product photos** to use accurate close-ups of the ear cushion thickness. This is causing a visible expectation mismatch in 14 separate reviews and will reduce returns if corrected.

2. **Reduce email support SLA from 24h to 48h stated SLA** — or hire one additional support agent. The gap between live chat and email satisfaction (4.6 vs. 3.1) is our biggest satisfaction driver. Target: bring email response time to ≤24h actual response.

3. **Introduce a size guide widget** on SHOE-4521 product pages. 8 reviews citing sizing issues suggests a static size chart is insufficient; an interactive guide will reduce returns and improve conversion.

## 5. Returns & Refunds Context

All refund requests during April were processed in line with our **30-day returns policy**. Of the 43 negative reviews, 17 originated from customers who had a return or refund interaction. Refund satisfaction rate: 78% (customers who rated their returns experience 4 or 5 stars).

Full returns data is available in the April Refund Log. Customers are reminded at point of purchase and in the post-purchase email that returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.
