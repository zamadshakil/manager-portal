6. INSIGHTS
==================================================
- Instagram Reels drove the highest volume of traffic and the best engagement.
  The short-form video format significantly outperformed static images (+2.1x CTR).
- Google Shopping performed below expectations for ELEC-3301, likely due to
  competitive bidding from larger retailers in the electronics category.
- Email newsletter had the highest conversion rate of all channels (8.2%),
  suggesting our subscriber list is highly purchase-intent.

==================================================
7. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR NEXT CAMPAIGN
==================================================
1. Increase Instagram Reels budget by 25% for the summer campaign (CAMP-2025-06).
   Based on this campaign, each $1,000 in Reels spend generated $5,900 in revenue.
2. Pause Google Shopping for ELEC-3301 and redirect that budget to KITC-0887
   and BAGS-1042 which have lower competition and higher organic search intent.
3. Segment the email list by past purchase category for the next campaign to
   improve relevance and target an email conversion rate of 10%+.
